TIMELINE ALIGNMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Year 0 (1836 game-start):
  • army_reserves + general_staff granted (one-time)
  • mdp_infamy_decay begins (runs to year 10)
  • expfight_warbump/warboost begins (short-war: 5yr / long-war: 10yr)
  • expmkt_industrialist_boost + coastal seed pulse begins (runs 4yr)
  • Interventionism law applied

Year 1-4:
  • expmkt.1 yearly coastal pulse checks for new coastal states, seeds them
  • expmkt_industrialist_boost runs throughout

Year 5:
  • SHORT-WAR expanders lose expfight_warbump (war footing expires)
  • LONG-WAR expanders still have expfight_warboost (continues to year 10)
  • expmkt_industrialist_boost expires (industrialist infrastructure is self-sustaining)

Year 10:
  • mdp_infamy_decay expires (infamy decay returns to base 5/yr)
  • LONG-WAR expanders lose expfight_warboost (war footing expires)
  • All expanders revert to vanilla: no conquest edge, normal infamy, normal morale
