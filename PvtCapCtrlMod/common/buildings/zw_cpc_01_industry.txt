# PvtCapCtrlMod — inject the state industry cap into every MANUFACTURING building.
# can_build_government gates the treasury/AI construction queue; can_build_private gates the
# private + foreign investment pool's NEW construction. Both (plus the auto-expand rule) are
# refused once the state hits its fixed pop-based cap (see zw_cpc_triggers.txt / zw_cpc_state_caps.txt).
# Scope of can_build_* is the STATE, so zw_trigger_under_industry_cap is called directly.
# (Auto-EXPANSION of existing buildings is handled separately via default_auto_expand_rule.)
