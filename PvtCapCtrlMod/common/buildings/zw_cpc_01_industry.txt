# PvtCapCtrlMod — inject the SEPARATE light / heavy industry caps into every manufacturing building.
# can_build_government gates the treasury/AI construction queue; can_build_private gates the
# private + foreign investment pool's NEW construction. Both (plus the auto-expand rule) are refused
# once the state hits the relevant fixed pop-based cap (see zw_cpc_triggers.txt / zw_cpc_values.txt /
# zw_cpc_state_caps.txt). can_build_* scope is the STATE, so the cap triggers are called directly.
# (Auto-EXPANSION of existing buildings is handled separately via default_auto_expand_rule.)
#
# Split (verified vs game files): LIGHT = bg_light_industry (incl. shipyards, a child of light);
# HEAVY = bg_heavy_industry + bg_military_industry. Each respects its OWN cap.
